About Catify

Catify is a free online tool that adds cat ears to any Minecraft skin and turns it into a catgirl. Load your skin with your Minecraft username or upload the PNG, choose an ear colour or a gradient, check the result in the 3D preview, and download the new skin. Everything happens in your browser and you don't need an account. Your skin is only uploaded if you choose to share it to the community gallery.

How to add cat ears to your Minecraft skin

  1. Type your Minecraft username and press Load, or upload your skin PNG.
  2. Pick a solid ear colour or a 2- or 3-colour fade, or press "Match my hair colour".
  3. Choose a colour for the inner ear.
  4. Press "Download skin" and upload the file on minecraft.net or in the Minecraft Launcher under Skins.

Questions

Does it work with slim (Alex) skins?

Yes. Catify detects slim and classic arms automatically, and you can switch between them under Arm width.

Will the ears show up in game?

Yes. The ears are painted into the skin itself, partly on the outer head layer, so they appear in vanilla Minecraft without mods. Make sure the hat layer is turned on in Skin Customization.

My hat layer covers the ears. What do I do?

Keep "Clear where the ears are" selected under the ear settings. It removes only the outer-layer pixels that sit on top of the ears and keeps the rest of your hat or hair.

Why is my old skin showing after a username lookup?

Skin services can take up to an hour to notice a skin change. Upload the PNG instead to use your newest skin right away.

What is the community gallery?

Press "Gallery" to browse cat-eared skins other people made, try one on or download it. To add yours, type a name (optional) under Texture and press Share. Shared skins are public.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Catify works on phones, tablets and computers. Drag to turn the model and pinch to zoom.
